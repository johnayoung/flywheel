  in silver on its quiet lakes.

  Then at the last, it meets the sea,
  and lets go of its history.
  It gives itself to something vast,
  no longer future, no more past.

  So when you stand beside its flow,
  remember all the river knows:
  that nothing stays, that all things move,
  and that letting go is how we love.
